# MVP Scope

## Included
- Party records with multiple roles
- Customer invoices with one total amount
- Payment instructions
- Payments and verification states
- Partial allocation of payments to invoices and instructions
- Confirmed and provisional balance views
- Unassigned invoice amount visibility
- Duplicate-reference and mismatch warnings
- Audit history for important events
- Persian RTL shell UI

## Deferred
- Full CRUD flows
- Sophisticated filtering and dashboards
- Reporting exports
- Bank integrations
- Accounting ledger features
- Inventory, warehouse, and product tracking
