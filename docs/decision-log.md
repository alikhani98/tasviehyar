# Decision Log

- The MVP stores invoice totals only, not invoice line items.
- Paper notebooks remain in use during the pilot.
- The primary initial data-entry user is a staff operator.
- The elderly manager initially uses the application mainly to view results.
- Stage 1 is party management only; invoice and payment workflows remain disabled.
- Reported and confirmed payments are separate states.
- Invoice instructions may be assigned gradually.
- Stage 3 invoices contain only one total amount and one customer.
- Stage 3 stores invoice lifecycle as active/cancelled instead of editable settlement status.
- Stage 3 uses cancellation instead of hard deletion for invoices.
- Stage 3 duplicate invoice detection is warning-based rather than blocking.
- Stage 3 does not store payment or balance state yet.
- Stage 4 payment instructions stay attached to invoices and do not store a redundant payer.
- Stage 4 instruction totals are derived from active records and must not exceed the invoice total.
- Stage 4 cancellation and restoration preserve instruction history instead of deleting records.
- Stage 5 records real payments separately from instructions so reported and confirmed states remain explicit.
- Stage 5 payment verification history is append-only.
- Stage 5 allocations can be split across invoices and instructions, but never exceed the payment amount.
- Stage 6 settlement balances are derived from the current active records and are not stored as editable truth.
- Stage 6 reconciliation highlights mismatches instead of silently fixing them.
- Stage 6 summary cards are navigation aids, not additional data entry surfaces.
- Stage 7 backups are local files and restore replaces the current local database.
- Stage 7 restore validation happens before any destructive write.
- Stage 7 backup history metadata is local-only and helps pilots recover safely.
- Stage 9 reports are read-only derivations of existing records and do not create stored summary tables.
- Stage 9 Excel exports are reports, not backups, and cannot be restored into TasviehYar.
- Stage 9 exported files contain sensitive financial information, while bank identifiers are masked by default in the UI.
- Stage 10 keeps one visual language across the shell and feature areas instead of introducing separate page styles.
- Stage 10 demo backups are generated separately from live backups so the demo flow does not mutate the user's real local history.
- Stage 10 demo data is fictional and isolated from the live IndexedDB database.
- Stage 10 demo backup restore is replace-all, not merge, just like the live backup workflow.
- No hard deletion of financial records.
- All money is stored in integer تومان values.
- No commit, push, or pull request is created without explicit instruction.
