# Product Brief

TasviehYar is a Persian, local-first settlement tool for a traditional pistachio wholesale business.

The pilot focuses first on party management, then on invoice totals, destination accounts, payment instructions, partial payments, verification states, outstanding balances, reconciliation summaries, backup safety, offline readiness, and auditability. The business keeps detailed notebook data outside the app for the time being.

The application is intentionally narrow. It does not aim to replace accounting, inventory, or ERP systems.
