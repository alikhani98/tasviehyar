# Product Brief

TasviehYar is a Persian, local-first settlement tool for a traditional pistachio wholesale business.

The pilot focuses first on party management, then on invoice totals, destination accounts, payment instructions, partial payments, verification states, outstanding balances, reconciliation summaries, operational reporting, backup safety, offline readiness, visual polish, and auditability. The business keeps detailed notebook data outside the app for the time being.

Stage 9 completed the read-only operational reports and Excel export layer.
Stage 10 completed the unified visual design pass and added a safe demo backup file for training and quick-start demos.
The checked-in demo backup is `demo/tasviehyar-demo-backup-v1.json`, and it exists only for demonstrations and validation.

The application is intentionally narrow. It does not aim to replace accounting, inventory, or ERP systems.
